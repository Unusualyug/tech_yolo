## Format and submission consequences

- Accepted text formats are (La)TeX, PDF, and HTML with supported image types.
  A standalone PDF generated by ReportLab is an accepted submission format.
  PDFs generated from TeX source should not be uploaded alongside that source.
- Figures must be embedded; omitted figures or external figure links are not
  accepted. File names may use only `a-z A-Z 0-9 _ + - . , =`, with no spaces.
  The current deliverable `tech_yolo_updated_research_paper.pdf` complies.
- arXiv metadata fields require ASCII. The title must not be all uppercase; the
  abstract field must not include the word “Abstract.” `scripts/check_arxiv_metadata.py`
  checks these mechanical properties.
- Suggested author metadata, to be pasted by the author only after the final
  paper rewrite:

  ```text
  Authors: Yug G. Lakhani (1)
  ((1) Department of Computer Engineering, Gyanmanjari Innovative University, Bhavnagar, India))
  ```

- arXiv expects authors to self-submit, grant a distribution license, and use a
  registered author account; endorsement may be required for a new category.
  The agent cannot click these author-only submission steps.
- The content-type policy says research articles should be complete final
  drafts and that proposals for future research are typically not accepted.
  The current protocol-only report with an unfilled preregistered measurement
  plan therefore remains **not submission-ready** until Experiments C→A→B, the
  live-app evidence, and any completed D/E work are reported. The title and
  abstract must be rewritten around real findings at that point.
